- Prices are what DropHawk last observed, not a live retailer quote. Treat them
  as recent rather than current, and link the user to the retailer to confirm.
- Coverage is PC components and related hardware sold by Australian retailers.
  A product no retailer in that set carries is not in the catalogue, and absence
  is not evidence it does not exist.
- Prices are in the currency each listing is sold in, so amounts across two
  currencies are not comparable and DropHawk will not rank them against
  each other.
- There is no public write API. Price monitors and email alerts need a signed-in
  account at https://drophawk.net/signup, which is a human's job, not an agent's.
